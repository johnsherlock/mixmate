# F08-S04 — Keep the AUX master visible in expanded groups

Feature: [F08](../features/F08.md)

Status: App 0.12.1 merged in [PR #75](https://github.com/johnsherlock/mixmate/pull/75) at `93ed34f` after affirmative review of final head `d8f36ce` and [passing CI](https://github.com/johnsherlock/mixmate/actions/runs/45447930334). Physical phone acceptance remains parked.

## Outcome

During rehearsal, expanding a group hid the AUX master. A performer needs to adjust the selected mix's overall level while working on its individual channels.

## Acceptance criteria

- The selected AUX mix's master stays visible and operable alongside the member faders when a group is expanded, including on phones in portrait and compact landscape.
- The master retains its existing fader, nudges, output meter, dB readout and control eligibility. Its value and target follow the selected logical AUX mix.
- Expanding, collapsing or switching a group sends zero writes and preserves the existing member-bank touch behavior.

## Validation

Check group navigation and the master target with fake transport. Verify layout and usability in portrait and compact landscape. Follow the [presentation contract](../iem-presentation.md) and the [AUX level controls](../iem-aux-level-controls.md).


## Delivery verification

- App 0.12.1 builds on the F08-S07 merge `a9165f5`. Local Node 26.13.1 and npm 11.20.0 passed `npm ci`, all 613 tests and `npm run build` with an 18-asset precache. CI uses Node 24.
- The UI/session regression uses fake mixer sockets. Expanding, switching and collapsing groups and swiping a member track send zero writes. The same AUX master, fader input, cap and meter remain mounted for the selected mix, and the received dB readout follows companion updates. A cap drag in an expanded group sends exactly the paired `a.8.mix` and `a.9.mix` writes. Selecting mono AUX 1 and opening the group sends nothing, then a deliberate nudge sends exactly one `a.0.mix` write. The companion sends only lifecycle frames.
- In portrait the master stays in a sticky side column alongside the three-column phone member bank. Compact landscape keeps the group master at the left of the horizontally scrolling bank and the AUX master at the right. Vertical nudges beside the fader free drag travel without changing their action. See the [presentation contract](../iem-presentation.md#f08-s04-aux-master-in-expanded-groups--2026-11-24).
- Chromium 157.0.8103.15 on macOS ran the Vite live entry with a 24-member saved group, synthetic stereo meter frame and fake sockets. Touch swipes from member tracks scrolled with zero writes at 320×568, 390×844 and 820×1180 portrait and 568×320 and 667×375 landscape. The portrait rail stayed in view through the last row. At 320×568 the existing header requires an initial page scroll to see the whole first row. Landscape kept both master controls in view during horizontal swipes. With the install hint visible at 568×320, the measured master, group and member track heights were 101, 95 and 69 CSS px. The stereo meter showed both sides. A deliberate master touch drag sent three AUX 9/10 pairs, and one nudge sent one pair. The displayed dB followed fake companion receipts.
- Physical phone and Ui24R verification remains a [device follow-up](../user-follow-up.md#2026-11-24-f08-s04-expanded-group-device-follow-up). The most recent root desk check received no HTTP response, and no physical phone was available for this story. No desk session or hardware adjustment occurred during these checks.

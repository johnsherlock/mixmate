Feature: [F07](../features/F07.md)
Status: Planned

## Outcome and scope

Verify snapshot list/recall/save protocols under ADR-0002 user authorization. Document exact narrowly scoped message forms, operation identifiers, types/ranges, capability/state prerequisites, confirmation, success/readback and interruption behavior; update active allowlist and guards in the reviewed implementation without a repeated feature-class permission request.

## Acceptance criteria

Listing is distinguished from mutation; exact commands and side effects are verified and reviewed under ADR-0002; the named snapshot recall/save exception does not permit arbitrary scene/configuration write paths; cancellation and disconnect behavior are defined; no mock snapshot labels become live facts.

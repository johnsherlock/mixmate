## Outcome and scope

Recover useful fader height by reducing persistent metadata and making full channel detail readily accessible by touch and keyboard.

## Acceptance criteria

- Move or condense normalized pan, mute/pre-post/processing and verbose VU metadata with discoverable accessible detail. Preserve pending/error/conflict/unavailable feedback, honest unknown mute state and source-meter post-fader meaning.
- Preserve three equal portrait columns, accepted compact landscape travel (96px at 844×390 and 93px at 844×300 baseline), readable labels, keyed strip/input/cap nodes and accessible full labels.
- Detail navigation/dialog ownership must not conflict with cap drag, double-track jump, single-touch/swipe scroll or keyboard release controls. Opening/context changes cancel according to the reviewed control contract.
- Fake/browser checks cover reachable detail, focus/keyboard/touch behavior, overflow, meter-only stable DOM and pending/error states. No mute, pan-direction, balance or DSP writer is introduced; physical touch qualification remains open.

# F03-S04: Verified meters and freshness

Feature: [F03](../features/F03.md)
Status: Planned
Depends on: F03-S03

## Outcome and scope

Decode evidenced VU2 frames safely and label meter tap points accurately.

## Acceptance criteria

Malformed/truncated/count-overflow packets are rejected; missing/expired meters differ from silence; stereo is evidence-based; source meters are not presented as measured AUX-send contribution; rendering is throttled; no synthetic or invented group meters.

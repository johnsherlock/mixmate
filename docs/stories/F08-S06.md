# F08-S06 — Dedicated Nudge view

Feature: [F08](../features/F08.md)

Status: User approved the design on 2026-11-24. The 0.11.0 preview merged as [PR #73](https://github.com/johnsherlock/mixmate/pull/73) at `00829e9`, with final review of `04a1f8c` and [passing CI](https://github.com/johnsherlock/mixmate/actions/runs/45442408882).

## Outcome

A performer can make quick level adjustments mid-song with large tap targets that are easy to hit while playing.

## Design preview

- Place **Nudge** immediately left of the group selectors in group view. Open a dedicated screen for the current AUX mix, with a clear way back to the same group.
- Show large, clearly separated decrease and increase buttons for the AUX master, every group, and the individual channels in the currently expanded group. Each card shows the target name and current fader dB where available. Group dB follows the existing loudest-member readout. Unequal stereo AUX and channel readouts show both sides with one dB unit and a dash for a missing side. The tooltip and screen-reader text identify the AUX number for each side.
- Use one grid of equal-sized cards, three across on phones and four across on tablets. Distinguish the AUX master, groups and channels visually.
- Review target size, spacing, readability, scrolling and return navigation in phone portrait and landscape. Preview taps provide visual feedback only and send zero writes.

Live adjustment is tracked in [F08-S07](F08-S07.md).

## Delivery verification

- App 0.11.0 on main baseline `0a6e801`. Local Node 26.13.1 and npm 11.20.0 passed `npm ci`, all 605 tests and `npm run build`. The production worker precaches 18 assets. CI uses Node 24.
- Chromium 157.0.8103.15 on macOS, using a local Vite fixture and synthetic mixer state, showed three cards across at 320×568, 375×667, 390×844, 568×320 and 844×390 and four across at 820×1180, 1024×575 and 1180×820. Cards had equal dimensions within pixel rounding, distinct AUX, group and channel accents, and a single-line name and dB readout. The measured channel buttons were at least 50×52 CSS pixels from 375 pixels wide and about 41×52 at 320×568. Both stereo numbers fit at 320×568 even for the long readout `-199.0/-198.9 dB`. The fixture also covered a missing left side, a missing right side and silence.
- Clicking every decrease and increase button left the displayed levels unchanged. The fixture had no mixer transport. The automated presentation test also checks that preview presses never enter the level gesture handler, received values refresh while open, and Faders preserves the expanded group's strip and scroll context. The browser check with a 24-channel expanded group verified that Faders restored focus to Nudge and preserved the page scroll in portrait and the horizontal fader-bank scroll in landscape. Automated checks cover parity between the group card and group fader during pending group and member adjustments.
- Browser evidence is desktop viewport emulation with synthetic data. Physical devices, touch and the desk were outside this preview check.

## Outcome and scope

Connect the receive-only IEM transport/store to master/send/pan readouts and actual stereo/source discovery.

## Acceptance criteria

Outbound transport accepts only INIT, ALIVE and heartbeat; no generic sender or setInputFader capability reaches IEM; partial/unknown/conflicting state is explicit; mixer strings use textContent; disconnect/hide/navigation clear state; no auto reconnect.

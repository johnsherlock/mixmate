Linked master commands at `0.399` and same-source pan commands at `0.001` applied, but no updated paths arrived on the sending socket before the 2.5-second deadline. Controls disarmed while the socket stayed live; an explicit fresh connection received the changed values. Immediate pan socket closure was not reproduced. App hiding during setup caused an intentional disconnect; do not assume this explains the user's earlier report.

Investigate current-firmware sender echo, precision and linked-path behavior; distinguish stale display, disarming, dialog closure and actual socket closure.

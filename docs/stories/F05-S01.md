## Outcome and scope

Implement the verification contract for the AUX level/group feature classes already authorized in ADR-0002. Document verified exact outbound forms, types/ranges, stereo/current-socket prerequisites, arming and interruption behavior; deliberately update AGENTS.md active allowlist and narrow guards in the reviewed feature implementation. No repeated feature-class approval request is required.

## Acceptance criteria

Exact paths/ranges and allowed message forms are evidence-backed and reviewed under the accepted authorization; per-target arming/disarming and current-socket prerequisites are agreed; source/output stereo behavior and non-atomic partial failure are defined; no queue/retry/replay or incoming-state-driven writes; input .mix is never an AUX fallback.

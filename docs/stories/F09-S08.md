# F09-S08: Groups to −∞, group strip tracking, GEQ handle keyboard, export removal and navigation placement

Feature: [F09](../features/F09.md)
Status: Merged — [PR #54](https://github.com/johnsherlock/mixmate/pull/54) at `9527f91` after independent review approval of `c978c5c`, passing CI and root live acceptance (PR record).
Depends on: F09-S07 merged at `dc371cb` (PR #53); user feedback and decisions of 2026-11-20

## User feedback (2026-11-20)

After testing F09-S07 on the desk the user reported:

1. "Moving the group faders should stop when one hits −∞, not −70 dB."
2. Moving a member fader inside an expanded group makes the group fader flicker "Level unavailable"; when the dragged member reaches −∞ the group fader disappears.
3. "We should be able to click on points 1, 2, 3 on the graph and move them up/down/left/right; this doesn't work." Root found pointer drags of handles work in Chromium, so this story improves robustness and adds keyboard moves.
4. The **Export settings** buttons in Edit Groups and the Connect live IEM dialog are not in the design and export little: remove the feature.
5. The group buttons and **All Channels** sit on the left after the mix name; the Demo puts them on the right, immediately left of **Mix EQ** / **Edit Groups**.

The two accepted F09-S07 text follow-ups are also fixed: the render-time restart hint was not visible in the status line, and `anchor.interrupted` could stay set after a no-move release or Escape.

## Root cause and decisions

1. **User decision (2026-11-20): group faders travel to −∞.** This supersedes the −70 dB group floor. A group applies one common delta in display-scale units (`positionToScale`/`scaleToPosition`), not raw dB. Above −70 dB that is identical to dB. In the bottom segment, members keep moving equal on-screen distance.
   - Up stops when the loudest member reaches position 1 (scale +10).
   - Down stops when the quietest member reaches position 0 (scale −75, −∞).
   - Groups may write position 0.
   - Silent members are valid finite anchors at −75: the group can rise, but it cannot go further down.
   - Anchors carry `scale`, not `db`. `GROUP_FLOOR_SCALE`/`GROUP_TOP_SCALE` replace `GROUP_FLOOR_DB`, and `groupTravel` returns `referenceScale`.
   - The desk fader law moved to `fader-law.js` (re-exported from `relative-groups.js`) to avoid a module cycle.
   - The display scale and single faders are unchanged.
2. **Group strip flicker.** There were two causes:
   - `referencePosition` dropped members at 0, which left no cap and showed "Level unavailable".
   - The strip's "unavailable" state followed `canEditLevel(group)`, which is legitimately false while a member gesture runs or its own echoes are outstanding.

   Now the cap is the maximum displayed member position: received, or this owner's requested value while its own member gesture or live steps are active, with 0 included. "Level unavailable" and the disabled state appear only when controls are absent or no member has a finite position. The session still refuses to begin a group gesture it cannot anchor, so presentation grants no authority.
3. **GEQ handles.**
   - Pressing a handle selects it, focuses it and grabs it. A **mouse** press on the empty graph within 24 px of a handle grabs the nearest one; touch/pen grabs come only from the handle-owned transparent r=24 hit circle so a background touch sends nothing (F09-S08 review). *Superseded in part by [F09-S10](F09-S10.md):* the whole graph svg is now `touch-action: none`, because Chrome ignores the handle's own value; a background touch no longer pans.
   - The r=24 hit circle and r=20 halo are the handle's hit targets (`pointer-events: visible`).
   - The SVG suppresses text selection, native drag and the callout (CSS, plus `dragstart`/`selectstart` preventDefault).
   - Arrow keys on a focused handle are a deliberate keyboard gesture through the same begin/preview/commit path. Left/Right moves ±1/62 of the x range and Up/Down moves ±0.5 dB; Shift is 4×. The gesture commits on keyup, and Escape or focus loss cancels it.
   - The graph's top and right margins are 22 px, so a handle centred at +15 dB or at 20 kHz keeps its whole halo inside the viewBox.
4. **Export removed (user decision 2026-11-20).**
   - Live app: the Export settings buttons and handlers are removed from the connection dialog and Edit Groups. Temporary saves now say the definition "will be lost when this page closes".
   - Settings / test: Export and Import backup are removed. **Reset local settings** and storage protection stay.
   - `parseSettingsBackup`, `settingsBackupText`, `exportSettingsBackup` and the import preview are deleted.
   - The repository schema and the v1→v2 read migration are unchanged.
5. **Navigation placement.** Live CSS used to force the first group button's start margin to 0, which left-aligned the buttons. The live group-switch container now uses `justify-content: flex-end`, as the Demo does, so wrapped rows stay right-aligned too. Active-state classes are unchanged.
6. **S07 follow-ups.**
   - The restart hint is also shown in a head badge (`.mm-live-eq-hint`), which is visible even where the status line under the graph is scrolled or clipped.
   - A cancel marks the anchor interrupted only if the gesture moved points.
   - A render-time check that finds the curve matching clears `interrupted`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Group drag down stops with the quietest member at −∞ (position 0), and the others keep their on-screen offsets. Drag up stops with the loudest at position 1. Above −70 dB, equal dB means equal distance.
- A group containing a member at −∞ can be moved up (that member rises from the bottom) but allows no downward travel.
- Dragging a member inside an expanded group never shows "Level unavailable" on the group strip:
  - The group cap follows the loudest displayed member, including the live requested value of the member being dragged.
  - Dragging a member to −∞ leaves the group available, with the cap at the loudest remaining member.
  - With all members at −∞, the cap sits at the bottom and the group can move up.
- GEQ points:
  - A pressed handle becomes selected and focused.
  - Arrow keys move it: Left/Right ≈1/62 of x, Up/Down 0.5 dB, Shift 4×. Changes are sent live and commit on keyup.
  - A press within 24 px of a handle grabs it.
  - Point 1 pinned at +15 dB still moves horizontally.
  - Handles at the ±15 or x edges stay fully inside the SVG.
- Edit Groups and the Connect live IEM dialog have no Export settings button. Settings / test has no export or import, while reset and storage protection still work.
- Live group buttons and All Channels are right-aligned immediately before Mix EQ / Edit Groups at desktop, tablet and phone portrait/landscape, with no horizontal page scroll.
- Unchanged:
  - wire forms, allowlist and typed guards (strict finite 0..1), literal factories;
  - the private writer plus receive-only companion, and own-echo rules;
  - keyed nodes, touch rules, phone portrait three-column banks;
  - Demo isolation and the `mockups/iem.html` reference.

## Validation

Fake transports and fake timers only.

- Group model tests:
  - one common scale delta over random dB, bottom-segment and silent members;
  - endpoints exactly 0 and 1;
  - equal dB above −70;
  - silent anchor blocks downward travel but rises.
- Session tests: the group bottom writes 0 for the quietest; the next gesture from −∞ allows only upward travel; a silent member arms.
- Presentation test: during a simulated member gesture the group strip never shows the placeholder or `data-unknown`; covers member at 0, all members at 0, and no finite position.
- GEQ tests:
  - keyboard moves with steps and Shift, commit on keyup, Escape cancel;
  - near-handle grab within 24 px and none at 30 px;
  - point 1 pinned at +15 moves x, with the halo inside the viewBox at the top and at 20 kHz;
  - the S07 follow-ups: no stale "interrupted" label, and the hint is visible in the status and the head.
- Export-removal tests in the navigation and lifecycle suites. Import/export lifecycle tests are removed, reset tests are kept, and the v1→v2 migration stays covered in `settings.test.js`.
- A CSS test checks the right-aligned group switches.
- `npm test` 426/426 pass; `npm run build` passes.
- Browser check by the story agent: Vite dev server in the desktop app's browser at 1280-wide, 375×812 and 844×390. Group buttons were injected into the disconnected live shell for layout only. They sit right-aligned before Mix EQ / Edit Groups, matching the Demo, with no horizontal scroll. No desk or mixer connection was used.

## Live acceptance: passed (root, 2026-11-20) — results in the PR record

- Group drag to the bottom puts the quietest member at −∞ while the others keep their spacing. A group with a member at −∞ moves up but not down. The Δ readout is plausible.
- Dragging a member up or down in an expanded group: the group cap tracks the loudest member and never flickers "Level unavailable". A member at −∞ does not hide the group fader.
- GEQ points: click or drag handles (including near misses) on desktop and touch; arrow and Shift+arrow keys on a focused handle; point 1 at +15 still moves sideways; L/R stay equal.
- No Export settings buttons appear in either dialog.
- Navigation buttons are right-aligned as in the Demo at desktop, tablet and phone sizes.

Physical Android/iOS/offline/sleep qualification remains [F08-S01](F08-S01.md).

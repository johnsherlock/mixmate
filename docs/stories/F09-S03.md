## Outcome and scope

Implement the reviewed F09-S02 model for existing AUX master/send level, relative-group and eligible pan controls, with coherent requested-versus-received presentation. This is a deliberate runtime-contract integration, not a deadline removal.

## Acceptance criteria

- Use only existing approved exact wire forms through narrow factories, strict typed guards, private current-socket owner and opaque owned gestures. A read-only factory remains literal; no generic sender or raw path/value-array authority.
- Keep actual individual received values distinct from requested markers and feedback. Repeated moves use only the ADR-defined safe owned baseline; avoid misleading snapback or pending deadlock without inventing receipt authority.
- Update AGENTS, applicable contracts and guards deliberately in the reviewed PR. Preserve explicit arming, deliberate release/keyup or supported preset intent, cancellation/disarming and no queue/retry/replay/rollback.
- Fake regressions cover no echo, precision differences, echoes, native changes/away-back, missing and partial paths, reentrancy, late/fresh generations, interruption, silent groups, baseline ownership and cached common-clamp intent. Prove all-path behavior and stopped partial dispatch.
- Preserve keyed nodes, cap drag/double-track jump/single-touch scroll, three-column portrait, compact landscape travel and offline/local assets. Do not add pan direction labels, linked-source/FX balance, EQ writes or other new command classes.

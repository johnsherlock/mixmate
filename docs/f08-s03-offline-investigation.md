### Why sender observation matters

`src/iem/session-core.js` owns socket generation, received observations, per-path receipts, revisions and control intent. `dispatchPlan` counts authorization separately from successful `send()` return; after synchronous dispatch it starts the confirmation deadline. `journal`, `finishReadback` and the typed level/pan guards require fresh post-authorization receipt for **every** path. INIT state received earlier, a live heartbeat or matching public snapshot cannot confirm an operation. A matching observation is not a correlated acknowledgement: inspected SETD has no transaction ID.

`src/iem/live-view.js` clears `preview` in `onCommit` and restores `model.position` from `projectLiveState` during render. Numeric readout stays received throughout. Thus a cap returning while PENDING is explainable without assuming the desk rejected the command. On missing receipt, `inspectConfirmation` calls control cancellation rather than socket stop. Transport can stay live while controls become OFF. Native unexpected values/topology/context changes can cancel earlier. Hiding invokes the controller's intentional disconnect and clears received state; it is a separate path.

Pan serialization normalizes to three decimals; master/send level retains numeric precision. Raw observations retain their actual precision; exact comparisons can invalidate a visually identical rounded value. The new precision diagnostic receives `.6010000001` after requesting `.601`: the native-change path disarms, retaining actual raw state. This is a **separate hypothesis** from the historical trial's absent updated frames; precision cannot explain a path never arriving. No epsilon, alternate socket authority, getter or request form has been invented.

The no-echo fixtures simulate a desk that applies intent and exposes it only on a later explicitly connected socket. They prove client behavior under that assumption, not that firmware always behaves this way. Old-generation callbacks cannot pollute the fresh session. Late same-socket readback updates actual observations without rearming. Partial linked receipt remains pending until deadline, then unconfirmed even if its partner arrives late. Existing suites cover native away/back, reentrant partial dispatch, selection, dialog/context changes, hide, disconnect, held timers, send throws and no retry/replay/rollback.
